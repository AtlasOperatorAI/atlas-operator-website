# Research: friendly small-business software sites (fetched Oct 20, 2026)

## Jobber (getjobber.com)
- Hero: one plain-language promise ("Run a stronger service business") + one primary button ("Start Free Trial").
- "No credit card required." directly under the CTA, repeated near every CTA.
- Lists the trades it serves by name (Cleaning, Construction, HVAC, Plumbing…) so visitors recognize themselves.
- Real photos of real owners; warm tone ("You've got this, and we've got your back").
- Free tools (calculators, templates) as an on-ramp.
- NOT borrowed: review counts, "400,000 pros", revenue-growth stats, testimonials (we have none and won't fake them).

## Housecall Pro (housecallpro.com)
- Short headline "Everything to run and grow your business" + "No credit card required."
- Outcome-based feature names ("Get paid", "Win work", "Build trust") rather than technical features.
- Visible human contact (phone + support email) in the closing section.
- NOT borrowed: 200K+ pros, % growth claims, testimonials.

## Square (squareup.com)
- Warm, local-business framing ("Make it big on your block").
- Product shown on real devices in real settings.
- Friendly "Nice to meet you" ask to tell them about your business: a soft, low-pressure contact.

## Patterns we use on site-v3
1. One-sentence plain promise + two buttons (primary orange "Tell us what you need", secondary "Try it free").
2. Check-mark reassurance row under the hero ("No credit card to try", "Works on any phone", "Friendly, real people"). From Jobber/HCP.
3. Naming the audience in the eyebrow (contractors, shops, local businesses) so owners see themselves. From Jobber's industry list.
4. Real photos of small-business owners + real app screenshots inside phone frames. From Jobber/Square.
5. Outcome headings, not features ("Less guessing. Fewer phone calls. Happier customers."). From HCP.
6. Numbered 3-step "How it works".
7. Trust band with verifiable facts only (LLC, address, real email, you own your app, real example) instead of stats/testimonials.
8. Beginner FAQ as tap-to-open questions.
9. Soft contact form ("A few sentences is plenty"), Square's "Nice to meet you" tone, with plain email offered too.
10. Free tools as the on-ramp (Jobber "Free Tools").
