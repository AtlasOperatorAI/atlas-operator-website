How it works

Rates are daily mid-market reference rates, all quoted against the euro. To convert A to B we use rate(B) ÷ rate(A) × amount. The latest rates are saved on your device, so the calculator keeps working offline and shows the date of the rates it is using.

Rates come from the free fawazahmed0 currency-api (CC0), with Frankfurter (European Central Bank reference rates) as a backup. For information only, not for trading. Banks, cards and exchange booths add fees and spreads, so what you pay will differ.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes. Open it once with a connection and the rates are saved on your device. Offline it uses those saved rates and shows "Rates as of" with their date.

How often are rates updated?

Once a day. Each time you open the calculator online it fetches the newest daily rates.

Why is my bank's rate different?

These are mid-market reference rates. Banks, card networks and exchange booths add a margin or fee on top.

How do I use travel mode?

Pick your home currency and the currency of the country you're visiting. Type a price you see and the home price appears in large type. Tap ⇅ to convert the other way. The quick table shows common amounts at a glance, and you can add a tip.
