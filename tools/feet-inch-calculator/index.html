    How it works

    Every measurement is converted to inches (feet × 12 + inches + fraction), the math is done in inches, and the answer is converted back to feet, inches and the nearest fraction you choose.

    Stairs: risers = total rise ÷ max riser, rounded up; treads = risers − 1; stringer = √(run² + rise²). Rafter: length = run × √(144 + pitch²) ÷ 12. Board feet: thickness × width × length (ft) ÷ 12. Always check local code before building stairs.

    FAQ

    How do I add feet and inches with fractions?

    Convert each measurement to inches, add them, then convert back. 5' 7 3/8" + 2' 6 3/4" = 67.375 + 30.75 = 98.125 in = 8' 2 1/8".

    How many stairs do I need for a given rise?

    Divide the total rise by the maximum riser height (7 3/4 in under the IRC) and round up to get the number of risers. Treads are one fewer than risers. A 108 in rise needs 14 risers of about 7 23/32 in and 13 treads.

    How do I calculate rafter length from pitch?

    Rafter length = run × √(144 + pitch²) ÷ 12, where pitch is the rise per 12 in of run. A 12 ft run at 6/12 gives about 13' 5".

    How do I calculate board feet?

    Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12. A 2×6 that is 8 ft long is 8 board feet (nominal).
