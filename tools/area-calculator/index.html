    Square footage guide

    Flooring, carpet, paint, sod, roofing and many other materials are sold by area, so a good square footage figure is the first step of almost any home project. Here's how to measure and calculate it without the usual slip-ups.

    Measuring a room

    Measure wall to wall at floor level along the longest straight runs. Write each number in feet and inches, such as 12' 6", and round to the nearest inch for flooring. Sketch the room on paper and write the measurements on the sketch. That makes it obvious when a closet or bay needs to be added separately.

    Rectangles

    Length × width. Convert inches to feet first by dividing by 12: 12' 6" × 10' 3" = 12.5 × 10.25 = 128.1 sq ft. That's 14.24 sq yd or 11.90 m².

    L-shapes and irregular rooms

    There are two equivalent ways. You can split the room into rectangles and add them, or measure the whole outline as a rectangle and subtract the missing corner. A 20' × 15' outline missing an 8' × 6' corner is 300 − 48 = 252 sq ft. Splitting it into a 12' × 15' piece (180) and an 8' × 9' piece (72) also gives 252.

    Triangles and circles

    A triangle is base × height ÷ 2, where the height is measured at a right angle to the base. A circle is π × diameter² ÷ 4, or π × radius². A 10 ft round patio is 3.1416 × 100 ÷ 4 = 78.5 sq ft.

    Note that a square yard is 9 sq ft, not 3: it's 3 ft × 3 ft. Carpet is often priced per square yard, so check which unit a quote uses (see sq ft to sq yd for carpet).

    Adding waste

    Materials that get cut need extra. A common allowance is around 10% for straight-laid flooring and tile and 15% or more for diagonal or patterned layouts. Use the manufacturer's guidance when it's given. 252 sq ft + 10% = 277.2 sq ft, and you round up to whole boxes.

    Worked example: a whole floor

    Say you're flooring a living room of 15' 4" × 12' plus an entry nook of 4' × 3' 6". The living room is 15.333 × 12 = 184 sq ft and the nook is 4 × 3.5 = 14 sq ft, so the net area is 198 sq ft. Add 10% waste: 217.8 sq ft. If the flooring comes in boxes covering 20 sq ft, you need 217.8 ÷ 20 = 10.9, so 11 boxes. In the calculator, add the two rooms as separate rectangles, type 10 in the waste box, and the total appears with waste included.

    Metric rooms

    Switch to Metric and type metres: a 4.2 × 3.6 m room is 15.12 m², which is 162.75 sq ft or 18.08 sq yd.

    How it works

    Each area is worked out in the unit you type (square inches for feet-inch input, square metres for metric) and converted to square feet, then all areas are added. Rectangle: length × width. Triangle: base × height ÷ 2. Circle: π × diameter² ÷ 4. L-shape: overall length × overall width minus the missing corner (its length × width).

    1 sq yd = 9 sq ft and 1 sq ft = 0.09290304 m². Waste adds a percentage on top of the total; cost is the total with waste × your price per sq ft. Measurements are typed like a tape: 12' 6", 12ft 6in, 150 (inches) or 3/4.

    FAQ

    How do I calculate square footage?

    Multiply length by width in feet. A 12' × 10' room is 120 sq ft. For inches, convert to feet first: 12' 6" is 12.5 ft, so 12' 6" × 10' is 125 sq ft.

    How do I find the square footage of an L-shaped room?

    Take the overall length × width as if it were a rectangle, then subtract the missing corner. A 20' × 15' outline missing an 8' × 6' corner is 300 − 48 = 252 sq ft.

    How do I convert square feet to square yards and square metres?

    Divide square feet by 9 for square yards, and multiply by 0.0929 for square metres. 252 sq ft = 28 sq yd = 23.41 m².

    How much extra flooring should I buy for waste?

    A common allowance is about 10% for straight lay and more for diagonal or herringbone patterns. Check the manufacturer's guidance. 252 sq ft plus 10% is 277.2 sq ft.

    How many square feet is a 12x12 room?

    12 × 12 = 144 sq ft, which is 16 square yards or about 13.38 m².

    How do I measure square footage of a room with a closet?

    Measure the main room and the closet as separate rectangles, work out each area, and add them together.
