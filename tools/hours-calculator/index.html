How it works

Type the start time, tap AM/PM if needed, then Next for the end time, break minutes and an optional pay rate. Hours update as you type, shown as h:mm (8:30) and decimal hours (8.50), which is what most timesheets and payroll use.

  • Overnight shifts: if the end time is earlier than the start time, the shift ended the next day. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8:00.
  • Breaks: unpaid break minutes are taken off the shift.
  • Pay: exact minutes × rate ÷ 60, rounded to the cent. 7:20 at $15/hr = $110.00.
  • Same start and end: counted as 0 hours, not 24.

FAQ

How do I turn minutes into decimal hours?

Divide minutes by 60. 15 min = 0.25, 20 min = 0.33, 30 min = 0.50, 45 min = 0.75.

Where is the weekly total?

Tap ☰ More, then Week total. Enter each day's start, end and break.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the calculator is saved on your device. Nothing you type leaves your device.
