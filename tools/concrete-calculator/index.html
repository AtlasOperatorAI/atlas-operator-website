    How it works

    Slab: length (ft) × width (ft) × thickness (in) ÷ 12 = cubic feet. Footing: length (ft) × width (in) ÷ 12 × depth (in) ÷ 12. Column: π × (diameter in ÷ 24)² × height (ft). Multiply by quantity, add waste, then divide by 27 for cubic yards.

    Bag counts use typical premixed yields: 80 lb ≈ 0.60 ft³, 60 lb ≈ 0.45 ft³, 40 lb ≈ 0.30 ft³, rounded up. Check the yield printed on your brand's bag.

    FAQ

    How do I calculate cubic yards of concrete?

    Multiply length (ft) by width (ft) by thickness (in) divided by 12 to get cubic feet, then divide by 27. A 10 × 10 ft slab 4 in thick is 33.3 cu ft, or about 1.23 cubic yards.

    How many 80 lb bags of concrete do I need?

    An 80 lb bag of premixed concrete yields about 0.60 cu ft, a 60 lb bag about 0.45 cu ft and a 40 lb bag about 0.30 cu ft. Divide your total cubic feet by the yield and round up. A 10 × 10 ft, 4 in slab needs about 56 bags of 80 lb.

    How much extra concrete should I order?

    Most contractors add 5 to 10 percent for spillage, uneven subgrade and form bowing. Use the waste field to add it.

    Bags or ready-mix?

    Bags are practical for small jobs up to roughly 1 cubic yard. Above that, ready-mix delivery is usually cheaper and faster. Check your supplier's minimum order.
