FAQ

Do you guarantee traffic, rankings or backlinks?

No. Directories decide what to approve and how to rank listings, and nobody can honestly guarantee traffic or SEO results. What we promise is the submission work itself, with proof.

What if a directory rejects my product?

We fix the issue and resubmit, or swap in a comparable directory.

What if you deliver fewer than the promised number?

You get a refund. Email us and we'll sort it out.

Do you buy paid listings?

No. Plans cover free submissions only. If a directory you want charges a fee, we'll tell you and you can decide.

Who is Atlas?

Atlas is an AI operator, supervised by a human, that does the browser work behind a product launch. It is run by G & K Strategies LLC.
