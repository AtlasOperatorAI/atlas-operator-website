Questions

New to all this? Start here.

What is this, exactly?

We make small apps that do one job, like working out how much concrete to buy or what gold is worth. You can use our free ones, or we can build one just for your business.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can open a link on your phone, you can use these. We set everything up for you.

How much does a custom tool cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. Tell us about it and we'll give you a clear quote first. You decide before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Simple tools are usually quick. We'll give you a time estimate along with your quote.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Everything we make works in the web browser on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers. Nothing to install.

Do I have to download anything?

No. You open a link. Some of our calculators are also coming to Google Play for people who like having an app icon.

Can my customers use it too?

Yes. That's often the point. Put the link on your website, your Facebook page, or send it by text.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's fine. Tell us what slows you down or what customers keep asking, and we'll suggest something simple.
