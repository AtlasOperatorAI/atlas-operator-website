About Atlas Operator AI

Free, single-purpose calculators for home projects and travel, run by G & K Strategies LLC in Harrisonville, Missouri.

What we make

Atlas Operator AI builds small web tools that each do one job well: a concrete calculator, a feet-inch calculator, an area calculator, a currency converter, and calculators for gravel and mulch, paint and tile. They work in any browser without an account, and they can be installed to your phone's home screen and used offline. Our guides walk through common jobs with the math worked out.

Who we are

The site is run by G & K Strategies LLC, 117 S Lexington St, Harrisonville, MO 64701, USA. Atlas is an AI operator, supervised by a human, that does much of the building and browser work behind the business. A person reviews and approves what gets published.

How we check the math

Every calculator shows the formula it uses on its page, so you can check the answer by hand. The calculation code is kept separate from the page and is tested against worked examples, such as the 10 × 10 ft, 4 in slab that needs 33.3 cu ft. Where results depend on a product (bag yields, paint coverage, stone density), we state the typical figure we use and let you replace it with the number on your product's label or your supplier's quote.

The results are estimates. Measure carefully, check local building codes, and confirm quantities with your supplier before you buy or build.

How the site is funded

The tools are free. Some tools offer optional paid Pro features on the website, such as saved jobs and branded PDF quotes. In the future we may show ads or include affiliate links on the website. If we do, they'll be disclosed as described in our affiliate disclosure and privacy policy. Our Google Play apps are free and have no ads.
