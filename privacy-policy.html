Site Analytics, Free Tools & Sign-ups

Cookie-free analytics. To count visits, each page sends a small request to our own analytics service, hosted on Railway. For each page view we record: the date and time, the page address (without anything after a “?”), the website that sent you (its name only, not the full address), any campaign tags in the link (source, medium, campaign), a device type (desktop, mobile or tablet) worked out from your browser’s description of itself, a two-letter country code if our hosting network supplies one, and a visitor code. The visitor code is made by scrambling your IP address and browser description with a secret key that changes every day. It lets us count how many different people visited on a given day, but not tell who you are or connect your visits across days. We do not store your IP address or browser description, we do not set cookies, and we do not sell this data. Requests that look like they come from automated programs are not counted. Some calculator pages also record a few tool events (which version of a page you were shown, and clicks on upgrade or waitlist buttons), with the same daily visitor code and campaign source. We currently keep this data until we delete it ourselves; there is no automatic deletion yet.

Tool sign-ups and waitlists. Some free tools offer Pro features. If you register to unlock them, or join a waitlist to be told when paid checkout opens, we collect what you enter: name, business name (optional), email and phone (optional), plus which tool you used and how you found us (referrer and UTM tags). We use this to give you access, to contact you about that tool and new tools, and to understand which offers work. We only collect it when you tick the consent box, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We also record anonymous button clicks on pricing options so we can compare offers.

Local storage. Our tools use your browser’s local storage (not cookies) to remember which pricing option you were shown, whether you unlocked Pro, and any jobs you save. This data stays on your device; saved jobs are not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Deletion. To have your sign-up details deleted, email atlas.operator.ai@gmail.com from the address you registered with and we will delete them.

Mobile Apps (Google Play)

This policy also covers our Android apps on Google Play, published by G & K Strategies LLC: Area Calculator (ai.atlasoperator.area), Concrete Calculator (ai.atlasoperator.concrete), Currency Calculator (ai.atlasoperator.currency), Feet-Inch Calculator (ai.atlasoperator.feetinch) and Percentage Calculator (ai.atlasoperator.percentage). Each app opens the matching free tool on atlasoperator.ai in app mode.

What the apps send. No account or sign-up is needed, and the apps show no sign-up forms, no ads, no affiliate links and no purchases. Each time a tool page opens, the app sends the same cookie-free analytics request described above to our own analytics service on Railway: the page path, the referrer (which identifies the app), any UTM tags, a device type (desktop, mobile or tablet), a two-letter country code if our hosting provider supplies one, and the daily-rotating visitor hash. We do not collect the advertising ID, any device identifier, your name, email, location or contacts, and we do not share this data with anyone. All requests use HTTPS. Because this analytics data cannot be linked to you, we cannot look it up or delete it for an individual.

Currency rates. The Currency Calculator downloads public exchange-rate files from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and Cloudflare Pages (currency-api.pages.dev). These requests contain no personal data from us, but like any web request those services receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

On your device. Your unit choices, favorite currencies and the last downloaded exchange rates are kept in local storage on your device and are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.
