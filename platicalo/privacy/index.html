Platícalo privacy policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the Platícalo browser extension and the server it uses. Platícalo is made by G & K Strategies LLC, doing business as Atlas Operator AI ("we", "us"), 117 S Lexington St, Harrisonville, MO 64701. Platícalo is free. For our website, see the Atlas Operator AI privacy policy.

What Platícalo does

Platícalo has one purpose: to translate chat messages between Spanish and English on web.whatsapp.com, when you ask it to. You can translate up to 10 messages a day.

You agree first

The first time you use Platícalo, a one-time consent screen explains what is sent and where. Nothing is sent, and no anonymous ID is created, until you agree.

In short: We never log message text or save it to disk. Translations stay in server memory for up to 24 hours, then are deleted. Your IP address is used only for rate limiting: kept in server memory, deleted within about 2 minutes, never logged.

Message text

  • What is sent: only the text of the messages you press Translate on. Platícalo never reads other chats, never sends messages and never touches the Send button.
  • Where it goes: to our translation server, hosted on Railway. From there it goes to Google's Gemini API, and the translation comes back to you.
  • On our server: message text is never logged and never written to disk. Translations are kept in server memory for up to 24 hours, looked up by a scrambled code (a SHA-256 hash) of the text. Expired entries are deleted every minute, so nothing outlives 24 hours plus about 1 minute. A server restart clears everything.
  • Google: The text you choose is sent to Google's Gemini API to translate it, under Google's API terms. We delete our copy within 24 hours. See Google's Gemini API terms.

IP address

Your IP address is used only for per-minute rate limiting, to prevent abuse. It is kept in server memory, and old entries are deleted every minute, so it is gone within about 2 minutes. It is never logged and never written to disk.

Anonymous ID

After you agree, our server creates a random, signed anonymous ID. It is saved in your browser and sent with each translation request. A Railway database (SQLite on a Railway volume) stores the anonymous ID, the plan ("free") and your daily translation count with its date, so we can apply the limit of up to 10 translations a day. No name, email, phone number or message content is stored.

On your device

Chrome extension storage holds the consent flag, your on/off setting and translation direction for each chat, and the anonymous ID. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than translation.
  • We do not use your data to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

Platícalo's use of information received from the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Processors

  • Railway – hosting and database.
  • Google – Gemini API, under Google's API terms.

Platícalo has no payments and collects no email addresses.

Deleting your data

  • Still installed: email atlas.operator.ai@gmail.com and include your anonymous ID, and we will delete that record. To find the ID: open chrome://extensions, find Platícalo, click service worker, then go to Application → Storage and copy the value of the key token. We plan to add an easier button later.
  • Already uninstalled (ID lost): the copy in your browser is gone. The record on our server holds only a random ID, the plan "free" and daily counts, with no name, email or other identifier. We cannot match it to you, and it cannot be used to identify you. Email us anyway and we will confirm this.

Children

Platícalo is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date.
