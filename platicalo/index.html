How to install

  1. Open the Platícalo listing in the Chrome Web Store on your computer.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Platícalo.
  4. Open web.whatsapp.com. The first time, read the short consent screen and press agree. Then turn on the switch for a chat and press Translate under a message.

Your privacy, in plain words

  • Before anything is sent, Platícalo shows a one-time screen that explains what happens and asks if you agree.
  • Only the text of the messages you choose to translate is sent, through our server to Google's Gemini API, under Google's API terms.
  • We never log message text or save it to disk. Translations stay in server memory for up to 24 hours, then are deleted. Your IP address is used only for rate limiting and is never logged.
  • Your first 5 translations need no email. For the rest of the free set you confirm your email with a 6-digit code. Payments for Pro are handled by Stripe.
