How to Calculate the Square Footage of a Room

Multiply length × width in feet. Convert inches to feet first (6 in = 0.5 ft), and add closets or bump-outs as separate rectangles.

Step 1: measure

Use a tape (or laser measure) along the floor from wall to wall, at the longest straight run in each direction. Rooms are rarely perfectly square, so measure both ends and use the larger number when you're buying material. Write it down in feet and inches.

Step 2: convert inches to feet

Divide the inches by 12: 3 in = 0.25 ft, 6 in = 0.5 ft, 9 in = 0.75 ft. So 12' 6" = 12.5 ft and 10' 3" = 10.25 ft. Don't type 12' 6" as 12.6, which is a different number. (The calculator above accepts feet-inch input directly.)

Step 3: multiply

12.5 × 10.25 = 128.1 sq ft. In square yards that's 128.1 ÷ 9 = 14.24, and in square metres it's × 0.0929 = 11.90.

Closets, alcoves and L-shaped rooms

Break the floor plan into rectangles, find each area and add them up. A 5' × 2' 6" closet is 12.5 sq ft, so the room plus closet is 140.6 sq ft. For an L-shape you can instead take the full outer rectangle and subtract the missing corner. The area calculator handles L-shapes, triangles and circles and adds as many areas as you need.

FAQ

How do I calculate square feet of a room?

Measure length and width in feet, convert any inches by dividing by 12, and multiply. 12' 6" × 10' 3" = 12.5 × 10.25 = 128.1 sq ft.

How many square feet is a 10x12 room?

120 sq ft, which is 13.33 sq yd.

Do I include closets?

For flooring, yes: measure each closet as its own rectangle and add it.
